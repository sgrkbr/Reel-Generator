Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

CoupleThings ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at sgrkbr.github.io/CoupleThings and an internal publishing tool used solely to manage our own brand-owned short-video accounts. This policy explains what data the tool handles when it interacts with third-party platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to:

  • The CoupleThings brand team members who use the internal publishing tool to upload content to our own platform accounts.
  • Visitors to this website.

The publishing tool is not a public product. It is not offered to end users. It authenticates only against accounts we own.

2. Data we access from TikTok

When a CoupleThings team member signs in to the publishing tool via TikTok Login Kit, we access the following data through the TikTok API, strictly for operating our own brand account:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): open ID, username, display name, avatar URL. Used to confirm the tool is connected to the correct brand account.
  • Content posting (video.publish, video.upload): the tool uploads pre-rendered MP4 videos that we produced ourselves to our own account, and reads upload status to track publishing.

We do not access, store, or process data belonging to any TikTok user other than the CoupleThings brand account owner.

3. How we use the data

  • To publish our own original animated videos to our own TikTok account.
  • To display the connected account name in our internal admin UI.
  • To log publishing status (success / failure / video ID) for our internal records.

4. How we store and protect data

  • OAuth access tokens are stored encrypted at rest in our private infrastructure.
  • Access is restricted to authorized CoupleThings team members.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit TikTok data to any third party.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or analytics beyond internal publishing logs.

5. Data retention

Access tokens are retained only as long as the brand account remains connected to the tool. Publishing logs (video ID, timestamp, status) are retained indefinitely for internal record-keeping. No personal data of other users is retained because none is collected.

6. Your rights

The brand account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from TikTok's connected-apps settings. Upon revocation, we delete the stored access token within 30 days.

7. Third-party services

The publishing tool integrates with TikTok, Instagram (Meta), and YouTube (Google) APIs. Use of those APIs is also governed by:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Instagram Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

8. Children

The publishing tool is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
